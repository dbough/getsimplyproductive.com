Are you looking for a free, simple way to manage your tasks without the complexity of a full-featured application? **Simply To-Do** is a Chrome extension that provides a distraction-free, easy-to-use checklist right in your browser. With just a few clicks, you can capture ideas, manage your daily list, and keep track of your progress. Here’s a step-by-step guide to getting started.

**Step 1: Install the Extension**

The first step is to add Simply Todo to your Chrome browser.

1. **Visit the Chrome Web Store:** Go to the Chrome Web Store and find the Simply Todo extension [here](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/simply-todo/jafjjbfkceeoagbcfnkiibfmkchcicce).  
2. **Add to Chrome:** Click the "Add to Chrome" button.  
3. **Pin the Icon:** Once installed, click the puzzle piece icon in your Chrome toolbar and pin the Simply To-Do icon for easy access.

### **Step 2: Add Your Tasks**

With the extension installed, you can start populating your list.

1. **Open Simply To-Do:** Click the pinned extension icon in your toolbar.  
2. **Type Your Task:** A simple text field will appear. Type your task into the field.  
3. **Add to List:** Press the `Enter` or `Return` key on your keyboard to add the task to your list.

### **Step 3: Manage and Prioritize Your Tasks**

Once you have a list of tasks, you can organize them to stay on track.

1. **Reorder Tasks:** To change the priority of a task, simply click and drag it up or down to your desired position. This helps you focus on the most important items first.  
2. **Complete a Task:** When you finish a task, click the checkbox next to it. The task will disappear, keeping your list clean and focused.

### **Step 4: Use Advanced Features**

Simply To-Do offers a few simple but powerful features to enhance your workflow.

1. **Keyboard Shortcut:** For quick task capture, use the keyboard shortcut `Control+Shift+S` (or `Command+Shift+S` on Mac). This will open the extension directly, allowing you to add tasks without having to click the icon.  
2. **Cross-Device Sync:** The extension automatically syncs your list across all your devices where you are signed in to your Chrome profile. This means your to-do list is always up-to-date, whether you're on a desktop, laptop, or another computer.  
3. **Dark Mode:** If you prefer a darker interface, you can toggle between light and dark modes by clicking the sun or moon icon in the extension window.  
4. **Track Your Progress:** The Simply To-Do icon in your toolbar will display a small badge with a number, which shows you how many tasks are remaining on your list.

**Final Thoughts**

Simply To-Do is a powerful tool because of its simplicity. It’s perfect for creating small, manageable lists of 3-5 important daily tasks or for managing the subtasks of a simple project. By keeping your lists short and focused, you can leverage this free extension to boost your productivity and stay organized.
